Questions

1.Is this an official BIU app?

No. Aim is built by a BIU student for BIU students. It uses the grading rules in the Faculty of Computing handbook. Always confirm anything official with your course adviser.

2.Which departments does it support?

Computer Science for now, full-time and part-time, 100 to 400 level. More departments are coming. Course reps, reach out!

3.Does it work with grades instead of scores?

Yes. The portal shows letter grades, so that’s what Aim uses. If you know a score (like 68), Aim converts it to BIU’s letter grade.

4.What about carryovers?

Retaken courses are supported. By default every attempt counts in your CGPA; if your adviser says only the latest counts, switch it in Settings.

5.Does Aim track me?

No cookies, no account, no ads. Aim counts anonymous visits (which screens get opened, roughly where from) using Vercel Web Analytics, so we know how many students it’s helping. Your name, grades and screenshots never leave your phone.

6.How do I install it?

Open Aim in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen”. You can even use your own photo as the app icon.
